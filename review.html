Review Guide — Read This First

You are checking whether map markers are placed at the correct waterfront locations (marinas, boat ramps, docks, landing stages) across Germany. Each marker was placed automatically and may be wrong.

Quick Decision Guide

1. Is the marker at a dock, marina, boat ramp, or landing stage on the water?
  → YES → Press 1 (Perfect)

2. Is the marker nearby (same town, same waterway) but not exactly at the facility?
  → YES → Drag the marker to the correct waterfront spot, then press 2 (Imprecise)

3. Is the marker in the wrong place entirely, or you cannot find any matching facility?
  → YES → Press 3 (Trash)

When in doubt: mark as Trash. Do not guess. Wrong data is worse than no data.

What To Do When You're Unsure

If the marker position is not obviously correct, use the side panel information to verify:

  1. Switch to satellite view (layer button, top-right of map). Look for docks, piers, or boats in the water near the marker.
  2. Check the side panel: the operator name ("Betreiber"), waterway name ("Gewaesser"), town ("Ort"), and address ("Strasse") tell you what should be at this location.
  3. Click "Google Maps" or "Street View" links in the panel to see real photos of the area.
  4. Click the "Google" search link to look up the facility and find its real address or website.
  5. Compare: Does the facility name, waterway, or address match what you see at or near the marker?
  6. If green-highlighted text appears in the "OSM Data" section, it means the map data name/phone/website matches — this is a strong confirmation.
  7. If you cannot confirm that the marker matches the entry → press 3 (Trash).

Classification Rules

Rating Key When to use
Perfect 1 Marker is directly at a waterfront facility (dock, marina, boat ramp, landing stage). You can see it on satellite or it matches the entry info.
Imprecise 2 Marker is close but not exact. First drag the marker to the correct spot, then press 2. Use this when you found the right facility but the marker was off by 50-500m.
Trash 3 Marker is clearly wrong (wrong town, wrong waterway, middle of nowhere) OR you cannot find any matching facility near the marker. When in doubt, use Trash.

Special Markers

  • APPROXIMATE Position was estimated, not matched to a real feature. Likely needs dragging or may be Trash.
  • DUPLICATE FLAG This entry shares its position with another entry. Check carefully — one may be correct and the other wrong.
  • DUPLICATE GROUP Multiple entries at the same spot. Purple markers on the map show the other entries. Click the linked name to jump between them.

Dragging the Marker

  • A grey ghost marker shows the original position.
  • To undo: click Reset in the coordinates row.
